Neon decks, live crowd, wet steel. The league’s broadcast stage — and where the season usually starts drawing blood.

Overgrown Ruins

Play

How the night runs

From the main menu: New Game opens match rules, then pilot/chassis pairs, then venue — or Campaign, five stories that unlock in order.

Versus

Local player vs player on one keyboard, or player vs AI on Easy, Normal, or Hard. Same roster, same arenas, clean best-of-three.

Campaign

Five pilot campaigns — Steel Heart, Redline, Ghost Orchid, Vanguard, Iron Web — told through ICN news between CPU matches. Finish one file to open the next.

Hangar & Jukebox

Inspect chassis notes in the hangar, read pilot dossiers, and spin the cabinet — including the track under your feet right now.

ICN Evening News

This is Vesna Holt

Between campaign matches the story breaks on Iron Circuit News — live monitor, typed caption, and Vesna on the plate. Season 2097 opens with Moravian Heavy Works on the brink, and ends somewhere the cameras were never supposed to go.
